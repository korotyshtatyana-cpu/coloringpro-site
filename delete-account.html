Account Deletion Request

App Name: ColoringPRO
Developer: Tatsiana Karatysh

How to delete your account

You can delete your account and all associated data directly within the ColoringPRO app:

  1. Open the ColoringPRO app.
  2. Go to the Profile or Settings screen.
  3. Tap the "Delete Account" button.
  4. Confirm the deletion.

What data will be deleted

When you delete your account, the following data will be permanently removed from our servers:

  • Your user profile (email, name, avatar).
  • All your saved coloring projects and drawings.
  • Your favorites list.
  • Any feedback or support messages you sent.
  • Your subscription and purchase history records.

Data retention

No personal data is retained after the account deletion process is complete. All data is deleted immediately and permanently.

If you have any issues deleting your account through the app, please contact us at korotyshtatyana@gmail.com.
